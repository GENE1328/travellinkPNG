About TravelLink PNG

Built to make finding help easier.

TravelLink PNG is an independent digital platform being developed to make it easier for Papua New Guineans to discover travel-related service providers. We're currently gathering interest from local businesses as we shape the experience.

Reviewed listings

Every business application is reviewed by EMDI administration before it appears publicly.

Clear, open profiles

Services, location and contact details are shown plainly so you know who you are speaking to.

Direct contact

Call, email or request an appointment straight from a provider profile.

Privacy-minded

Please do not enter passport numbers, scans or financial information on this platform.

!

Important to knowEMDI is not a government website and does not officially process passport or visa applications. Providers offer independent services; no faster passport approvals are promised.
